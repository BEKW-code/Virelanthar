E-001 | ACTIVE | INCORRECT TENSE TRANSLATION

FILE: Virelanthar definitive.MD

Original sentence:
Mi ya se makel ei navor.

Incorrect English:
I have repaired the machine.

Incorrect French:
J’ai réparé la machine.

CORRECT INTERPRETATION:
English: I had repaired the machine.
French: J'avais réparé la machine.

REASON:
The established standard uses ya + se for a completed action in
the past. Compare "Mi ya se sova" = "I had sung".

The Virelanthar sentence itself is correct.
Do NOT alter ya, se, or makel.

E-004 | ACTIVE | MARKDOWN METADATA STRUCTURE

FILES:
Virelanthar definitive.MD
Handoff.md

Both begin with an opening --- metadata delimiter but place
ordinary document text before closing the metadata section.

Strict Markdown/frontmatter parsers may misinterpret this.

INTENDED INTERPRETATION:
The metadata ends after the license: field.
The following VIRELANTHAR heading begins the document body.

This addendum explains the intended structure but cannot fix
a strict parser.

If rendering or automated metadata parsing fails, the actual
Markdown file needs a closing --- after its license: line.

This is a formatting issue, NOT a language change.

E-006 | DECISION PENDING | THREE NEW GRAMMAR PARTICLES

FILE:
VIRELANTHAR SPEAKING & RESONANCE TOOLKIT.txt

The toolkit proposes:

vara = possibility / maybe
turi = imagined or hypothetical scenario
rila = relative clause: who / which / that

These are documented NEW proposals.

They are not among the original 346 entries in Lexicon.json.

They must not be represented as ancient or previously established words.

Until approved, translators should identify them as documented
toolkit proposals.

If Keth'ar ratifies them, the machine-readable dictionary should
eventually record their functions.

Do not silently promote proposed forms into canon.


E-007 | ACTIVE | MEANINGFUL COMPOSITION, NOT WORD PADDING

Established derivations follow their documented prefix/suffix rules.

The speaking toolkit also proposes transparent noun-noun composition.

A compound must preserve the recognizable meaning of its components.

The relationship between those components must be understandable
and useful.

Not every possible combination deserves its own dictionary entry.

The earlier experiment generating 77,777 mechanically combined
forms was rejected as redundant word-count padding.

IT IS NOT AN ACCEPTED VOCABULARY RELEASE.

A language grows by allowing meaningful expression, not by
manufacturing thousands of unnecessary synonyms.

Do not repeat this mistake.

============================================================
PERMANENT USAGE GUARDRAILS
============================================================

1. Preserve established vocabulary and sacred formulas.

2. Preserve the distinct meanings of tense, aspect, negation,
   agency, resonance, intention and speaker perspective.

3. Multiple expressions of the same underlying idea are welcome.
   There is no requirement for every thought to follow one
   rigid rhetorical structure.

4. Ordinary speech, poetic speech, emotional speech, technical
   speech and High Speech may differ in presentation while
   preserving recoverable meaning.

5. Resonance may affect emphasis, performance, emotional framing,
   shared intention and musical response.

6. A poetic image or resonance flourish does not automatically
   redefine the literal meaning of a word.

7. Prefer established roots and meaningful combinations before
   proposing entirely new vocabulary.

8. Do not invent translations for unfamiliar words.
   Mark genuinely unknown forms as [UNKNOWN: exact form].

9. Virelanthar and Vaeltharyn are independent languages.
   Respect Zoé's vocabulary, grammar, authorship and translations.

10. Do not confuse proposed extensions, historical drafts,
    established canon, generated examples, or artistic adaptations.
