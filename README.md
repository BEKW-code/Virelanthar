
# Virelanthar
The Living Tongue of Light Remembered

A compositional language of meaning, resonance, intention, and expressive speech.

Creator and final editorial authority: Keth'ar Molishé Nuvah'el.

## Authoritative sources

1. `Virelanthar definitive.MD` — Established grammar, phonetics, sacred Pillars, numbers, and original standard.
2. `VIRELANTHAR SPEAKING & RESONANCE TOOLKIT.txt` — Current speaking toolkit, flexible expression, resonance, and clearly marked extensions.
3. `Lexicon.json` — Machine-readable dictionary of 346 established entries.

## Additional material

- `7777.txt` — 677 proposed words and 7,100 practice sentences. NOT automatically canon.
- `ChipieVirelanthar.md` — French-language introduction for Zoé.
- `Handoff.md` — Historical source audit containing obsolete and conflicting drafts. NOT current grammar authority.

## Rules for readers and AI translators

- Preserve established forms, meanings, and grammar.
- Read the definitive standard together with the speaking toolkit.
- Distinguish established vocabulary from proposed additions.
- Build new expressions compositionally; do not manufacture arbitrary words.
- Never invent translations for unknown forms.
- Preserve the Four Pillars and sacred closers unchanged.
- Respect Virelanthar and Vaeltharyn as independent languages.
- Prefer precise meaning over dictionary size.

A language grows through meaningful use, not inflated word counts.
